# v0.6.2

Blog Atom feeds now carry the whole post, clean enough to syndicate.

- Each entry includes the full article in `<content>`, not just the
  one-line summary. Importing a feed into dev.to, Medium, or a reader
  brings over the entire post and links its canonical URL back here.
- The body is rendered from the Markdoc source through a separate feed
  profile, not scraped from the page. Headings are plain text, the
  copy-link icons and search weights are gone, and the page wrapper is
  dropped. Galleries, heroes, avatars, and screenshots become plain
  images; mermaid diagrams become their source in a code block.
- Set `PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to make every feed and entry link absolute, body
  images included. Language prefixes and `BASE_PATH` are handled. Without
  it the feed still serves, but its links stay relative and the server
  says so at startup.
- Feeds are rendered once at startup and served from cache.
- The output is valid Atom 1.0 and passes the W3C Feed Validator.
- The Docker page gains a Compose example, and the reference now states
  that `PUBLIC_SITE_URL` belongs on every deployment.
