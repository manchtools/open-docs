# v0.5.0

Blog mode. Any section becomes a blog by setting `blog: true` on its
`index.md`; docs and any number of blogs share one container. This
changelog is such a section.

- Posts are pages with `date: YYYY-MM-DD` in frontmatter, sorted newest
  first. `draft: true` posts serve in dev only.
- The section index gets a generated listing: cover, localized date,
  reading time, author, description, tags.
- Authors are pages. `author: /blog/authors/ada` resolves the name,
  image, bio, and link from an ordinary page, in post frontmatter and
  in the `{% avatar %}` block alike.
- New blocks, usable on any page: `{% hero %}`, `{% avatar %}` (overlaps
  a preceding hero by half its image), `{% quote %}`, `{% gallery %}`.
- A post that starts with a hero derives its listing and social-card
  image from it; `cover:` frontmatter remains available.
- Tag pages under `/<section>/tags/<tag>` and an Atom feed per section
  and language at `/<section>/feed.xml`.
- Posts emit `article:published_time`, `article:author`, and their
  cover as the social-card image.
- The lightbox pages through a page's images with buttons and arrow
  keys. Heading copy buttons are faintly visible at rest; blog indexes
  add a feed copy button.
